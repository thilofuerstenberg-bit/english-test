The Case of the Missing Wallet

A shop assistant saw a tall person in a red hoodie near the changing rooms. The security camera showed that the person picked up a wallet from a bench and walked to the information desk. At 4:35, Mia asked the desk for help. The assistant handed her the wallet. Nothing was missing.
